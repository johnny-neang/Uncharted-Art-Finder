    About this brief

    Compiled by UpperCloud Studio for Johnny & the Arden Fair UnchARTed program. Each entry is graded on a four-axis fit score (style, scale, audience, availability) and accompanied by two visual references from the artist's recent portfolio.

    Field Brief · No. 014 · Cycle 2026

    How to use this

    Filter by tier or medium to narrow shortlists. Each card links out to the artist's primary portfolio. The directory is fully self-contained — open it offline, print it, or share the file.

    Visual references are stylized previews. Replace with real photographs by running python inject_images.py with images placed in local_files/.
